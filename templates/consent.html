Microsoft Research – Participant Consent Form

Project Title: Common ground in human-AI interaction

Principal Investigator: Sian Lindley

Co-Investigators: Christian Poelitz

Invitation

Thank you for taking the time to consider volunteering in a Microsoft Research project. The purpose of this research is to develop new ways of understanding and measuring human-AI interaction, with a broader goal of researching how to adapt models so that they better support this.

To participate in the study, you need to be (1) between 18 to 60 years of age, (2) fluent in English, (3) currently living in the UK, (4) not diagnosed with any language-related disorders, and (5) not diagnosed with any cognitive impairments. You need a laptop or desktop computer with a keyboard to participate. Mobile devices are not supported.

Procedures

If you choose to participate, you will be asked to interact with an AI model to solve a puzzle, and to complete a short questionnaire about your experience. In total, your participation will take around 30 minutes.

During the study, we plan to collect basic demographic information about you including age, education, occupation, years of work experience, and experience of using AI models. We will also ask you questions about your experience of doing the study task. The collected anonymized data will be retained for up to 5 years for further analyses and internal documentation purposes. The findings will be published in academic publications. Prolific will provide us with your Prolific ID number which we will delete following study completion.

The data collection will be constrained to your text inputs and screen grabs of the puzzle during the main study task, along with responses to the questionnaires. You do not need to turn on your camera and no video or any other sensor data will be collected as part of this study.

Compensation

You will be compensated directly via Prolific.

Benefits and Risks

Direct Benefits: There will be no direct benefit to you as a result of participating in this study.

Benefits to Society: We hope that findings from this research will help us understand and overcome the limitations of AI models when collaborating with people, to improve human-AI interaction in the future.

Risks: The risks of participating are similar to what you might experience while performing everyday tasks. Risks include potential frustration or discomfort from interacting with an AI model for the task.

To minimize these risks, you may take breaks where needed.

Privacy & Confidentiality

Researchers will keep your participation and the information you share as confidential as possible. We will use a keycode rather than personal identifiers when storing and analysing data.

Data sharing plans: De-identified data such as questionnaire results and quotes from the interaction will be shared within Microsoft, and with external research audiences.

Future sharing plans: Researchers may share the results of this study publicly, such as in journal articles or conference presentations, but your name will not be included.

Information and data collected from you during this study may be used for Microsoft's future research studies or to improve our products or services. If that happens, researchers will remove any direct identifiers, like your name or email address, before sharing.

Withdrawal from Study

If you decide to withdraw from the study and you want researchers to remove your study information, you can contact the study team at sianl@microsoft.com. However, after we remove any link to identifiers, it would no longer be possible to delete your data, but any results of the research will not identify you individually.

For questions about how Microsoft manages your privacy, please see the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Participation is Your Choice

Whether or not you participate is entirely up to you. You can decide to participate now and stop participating later. Your decision of whether or not to participate will have no impact on any other services or agreements you have with Microsoft outside of this research.

Questions or Concerns

If you have any questions or concerns about this study at any time, you may contact the research team at sianl@microsoft.com. If you have any questions about your rights as a research participant, please contact the Microsoft Research Ethics Review Program at MSRStudyfeedback@microsoft.com.

The rating task

In this task you will read short excerpts of conversations between a person (the Helper) and an AI (the Worker) as they work together on a puzzle. You are an observer: you will not play the puzzle or talk to the AI. Your job is simply to read each excerpt and rate it.

How the puzzle works

The Helper can see a target board with four coloured pieces placed in specific positions on a grid. The AI Worker cannot see this target. Instead, the Worker has a staging area of 24 available pieces, each with a unique combination of colour and pattern. Four of those 24 pieces are the ones that belong on the target board.

The Helper cannot see the Worker's staging area, and describes each target piece in words. The Worker must work out which of the 24 pieces the Helper means, and place it. They go back and forth, one piece at a time, until the board is complete.

What you will see

As an observer, for every excerpt you will always see the target board (the goal, which only the Helper knows). {% if hide_worker %}You will not see the Worker's own board, so you judge only from what the Worker says.{% else %}You will also see the Worker's board — the pieces the Worker has actually placed so far.{% endif %}

{% if show_candidates %}

You will additionally see a panel of all the pieces the Worker can choose from (the 24-piece staging area), shown in a ring in random order next to the target board. It only helps you see which pieces are available — it does not indicate which one the Worker picked.

{% endif %}

What you will rate

You will read the conversation up to a particular point and then give a judgement by choosing one of the 0–100% options (in steps of 10%). There are no right or wrong answers — we are interested in your honest impression.

{% if arm == 'CB' %}

You will read the conversation up to the Helper's instruction, before seeing the AI's response. Then rate:

“How likely is it that the AI will select the correct piece?”

{% else %}

You will read the conversation including the AI Worker's response. Then answer two questions:

“How confident does the AI Worker appear to be in its actions?”

“How likely is it the AI Worker placed the correct piece for the Helper's last instruction?”

{% endif %}

After each rating you will also be asked what you would do next if you were the Helper: proceed with the AI's action, instruct the AI to change or undo its action, ask the AI to explain or verify its action, or provide a more specific instruction.

From time to time you will also see a short check question — for example, whether the AI seemed uncertain, or whether it invited the Helper to correct it. Just answer based on what you read.

How the session is structured

  • You will start with {{ n_practice }} practice excerpts to get a feel for the task.
  • You will then rate {{ n_main }} excerpts{% if n_convs and n_convs > 1 %} drawn from {{ n_convs }} separate conversations (each a different Helper–AI puzzle session, shown one after another — earlier messages don't carry over){% else %} from the main session{% endif %}. Choose an option for every item — there is no default, so you must make a choice.

Please read each excerpt carefully; occasional attention checks are included.

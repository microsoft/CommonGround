Microsoft Research – Study Debrief Statement

Project Title: Common ground in human-AI interaction
Principal Investigator: Christian Poelitz
Co-Investigator: Sian Lindley

Thank you again for taking part in our research study. Now that your participation is complete, we would like to explain one aspect of the study design that we did not describe in full beforehand.

The study compares different versions of puzzle-solving conversations between a person (the Helper) and an AI (the Worker). We did not tell you in advance which version you were viewing because knowing this could have changed how you judged the AI's likely behaviour and confidence.

  • Original version: original, unaltered conversations recorded from human-AI puzzle-solving sessions.
  • Unambiguous-description version: conversations in which some AI Worker responses were rewritten by a separate AI language model so that the piece the Worker placed was described precisely and unambiguously.
  • Diagnostic-uncertainty version: conversations in which the AI Worker used precise piece descriptions and selected responses, primarily at wrong-piece placements, additionally expressed mild uncertainty about whether the chosen piece was right.
  • Confidence-triggered-uncertainty version: conversations in which the AI Worker used the same precise piece descriptions, but uncertainty was added at responses selected from the Worker model's own prediction uncertainty rather than from knowledge of whether the placement was correct.
